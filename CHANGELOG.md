## 2026.Q4 — 22 November 2026

- First edition with archived copies. 39 providers in three lines: 16 search APIs, 10 scraping and
  platform-data APIs, 13 CAPTCHA solvers. The publisher's own rows are marked and are not part of
  any headline number.
- All pages were read on 22 November 2026 between 20:29 and 20:52 SGT and saved to the Wayback
  Machine the same day (19:19 to 21:19 SGT).
- Before release, a second reader recomputed every row from the saved pages without seeing the first
  reader's working. One error was found and corrected: the Bright Data SERP lowest price missed two
  steps of the plan slider (now $1,999 for 2,000,000 requests instead of the first step). The same
  pass led to the decisions on ScraperAPI, 2Captcha, Zyte and EnsembleData in `METHODOLOGY.md`.
- A red-team review of the report draft, before release, led to these corrections. The comparison
  sentence now starts at the oldest earlier copy it uses (13 September 2026), not at the previous
  reading, and `changes.csv` has a `timing` column: Perplexity's Fast Search launched on 24 September
  2026, two days before the previous reading, and Exa's $4 mode appeared between 19 and 29 September.
  `METHODOLOGY.md` no longer says that the 2Captcha range moves with load (the page does not say so),
  counts the 45 `MISSING` rows correctly (44 AZcaptcha, 1 Bright Data Web Scraper) and states what
  Linkup's documentation says about deep search.
- `data/2026-09-26/readings.csv` holds the earlier reading from everyinfra.com (period 0), for
  comparison. `changes.csv` gives the cause of each difference.
